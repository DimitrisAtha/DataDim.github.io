What I bring

Insight a business can use, not just dashboards

Four things I do on every project, whatever the tool.

Answer the real question

Each report page answers one management question. The title states the answer, and a measure writes the insight sentence, so it stays right after every refresh.

Make comparisons fair

Same months against same months, rates in percentage points, every group measured on equal terms. No flattering numbers that fall apart in the meeting.

Find the risk behind the average

Averages hide things. Before I call something growth, I check how much of it depends on one client, one region or one season.

Automate the repeat work

Pipelines that pick up next month's file on their own, with checks that tie every total back to the source. A monthly update should be a refresh, not a rebuild.

How I think

My approach to an analytics problem

The same six steps, from the first question to the monthly refresh. The examples come from the Branch Performance case study.

  1. 01

    Start from the decision

    Who reads it, and what will they decide? Each page gets one question, and the visuals are chosen to answer it.

    Example: "Are we growing, on the same months as last year?"

  2. 02

    Check the data before the visuals

    Totals tie to the source, gaps are counted and named, and mappings are taken from the data, never guessed.

    Example: two gaps in the source found and sent to the data owner before the meeting.

  3. 03

    Compare like with like

    Same months, same time to mature, rates in percentage points. A fair number is worth more than an exciting one.

    Example: a product adoption jump of 3x became a fair 2x.

  4. 04

    Look behind the average

    Concentration, outliers and seasonality change the story, so I test them before anything goes on a slide.

    Example: one client behind a large part of a year's growth.

  5. 05

    Say what it means

    The title is the answer, the "so what" is written down, and the next step has a name.

    Example: presenter notes for every visual: what it shows, how it works, what to say.

  6. 06

    Automate the repeat

    Next month should take a refresh, not a rebuild. Inputs are read by name, checks run on their own.

    Example: new monthly sheets are picked up automatically by the model.
